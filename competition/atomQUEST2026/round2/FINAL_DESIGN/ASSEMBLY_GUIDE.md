# Assembly guide

## Load path

Ceiling plate -> downrod -> stationary yoke -> outer tilt axis (X) -> outer ring -> inner tilt axis (Y) -> inner cradle -> motor adapter -> original motor -> original rotor -> blades.

The ceiling load never passes through a printed part alone: the downrod is a 25 mm metal tube through the printed shroud.

## Sequence

1. Fit the motor adapter to the original motor using **measured** hole positions (currently a placeholder 4 x 4.5 mm on a 90 mm PCD).
2. Bolt the adapter to the inner cradle platform.
3. Insert the Y pivot bushings, close the outer ring around the cradle, insert the X trunnions.
4. Fit both servo brackets, then the servo horns to the pivots.
5. Mount the yoke on the downrod, thread the cable through the collar.
6. Fit the shroud, then the ceiling plate.
7. Clamp the hub on the original rotor flange and fit the three blades.

## Service

Blades come off individually (3 x M4). The cover lifts off the cradle without disturbing the tilt axes. The ring can be split for motor service once the trunnions are withdrawn.
