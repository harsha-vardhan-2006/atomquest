## STEP re-import (geometric equivalence)

Both checks were run: the text check above, and a real re-import of every STEP with `ISldWorks::LoadFile4` into a fresh part document, where the solid-body count and bounding box were measured and compared with `part_measurements.csv`. Results are in `07_ENGINEERING_CALCULATIONS/step_reimport.csv`.

- 18 of 18 STEP files re-import as exactly one solid body.
- 17 of 18 match their native part bounding box within 0.5 mm.
- **FD_Blade.STEP does not match to that tolerance**: the spline-lofted blade re-imports 6.7 mm shorter at the tip and 1.3 mm thinner (1.2 % and 1.7 % of its extents), giving an effective tip radius of about 693 mm instead of 700 mm. Treat the exported blade as a review geometry, not as the manufacturing definition.
- Volume is deliberately excluded from the comparison: an imported STEP body reports mass properties on a different basis from a native body (the downrod imports with an identical 25 x 25 x 232 mm box but a volume 1000x its native 48.1 cm3), so a volume test would be false.
