# Original hardware measurement checklist

None of the interface dimensions below are verified. Measure with a vernier/digital caliper (0.02 mm) and record each value with a photo of the instrument reading.

| # | dimension | how |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | motor body diameter and length | caliper across the can, and end-to-end excluding the shaft |
| 2 | mounting hole pattern | centre-to-centre of the two nearest holes and the bolt circle |
| 3 | mounting hole diameter and depth | pin gauges or caliper |
| 4 | shaft diameter, exposed length, any flat or thread | caliper + thread pitch gauge |
| 5 | bearing bore and width, seated depth | caliper in situ |
| 6 | rotor flange diameter and existing blade fixing | caliper, count and pitch of screws |
| 7 | rotor axial position relative to the mounting face | height gauge |
| 8 | motor mass | kitchen/postal scale, 1 g resolution |
| 9 | nameplate voltage, current, speed | photograph the label |
| 10 | connector and wire exit position | outline sketch with distances |
